Our Mission

BharatTV was built with a simple goal: make Indian television accessible to everyone, everywhere — for free. Whether you're an Indian living abroad missing your favourite news channel, a cricket fan wanting to catch live matches, or simply looking for entertainment in your regional language — BharatTV has you covered.

What We Offer

BharatTV aggregates over 19,000 publicly available live TV channels from India and around the world. Our platform provides:

  • Comprehensive Coverage: News, Sports, Entertainment, Movies, Music, Kids, Education, Devotional, Documentary, Lifestyle, Travel, and Science channels.
  • Regional Languages: Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Urdu, Bhojpuri, and more.
  • Multi-Country Support: Channels from India, USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Australia.
  • Clean Interface: Easy search, filters by language/category/country, dark and light themes, theater mode, and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Zero Barriers: No signup, no login, no downloads, no payment. Just open and watch.

How It Works

BharatTV uses publicly available IPTV stream data from the open-source iptv-org project. We do not host or store any video content. Our platform simply provides a user-friendly interface to browse and play these publicly available streams using HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) technology.
